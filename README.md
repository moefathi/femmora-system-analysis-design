# Femmora – E-Commerce System Analysis & Design

## Project Overview
This project focuses on the analysis and design of an e-commerce system for **Femmora**, a Jordanian women's fashion business that currently sells dresses through Instagram.

The proposed system aims to replace the manual Instagram-based ordering process with a web-based platform that supports product browsing, customer accounts, order management, inventory tracking, and business reporting.

## Business Problem
Femmora's existing Instagram-based process creates several operational challenges:
- Difficult product and inventory management.
- Orders managed manually through direct messages.
- Limited order tracking for customers.
- No centralized customer database or order history.
- Limited sales analytics and performance reporting.
- Difficulty handling increasing order volumes.

## Proposed Solution
A web-based e-commerce system designed to:
- Allow customers to browse and search products.
- Display product details and stock availability.
- Support customer accounts and order history.
- Provide shopping cart and checkout functionality.
- Support multiple payment methods.
- Allow customers to track order status.
- Enable administrators to manage products, orders, inventory, and customers.
- Provide sales reports and business performance information.

## Feasibility Analysis
The project evaluates the proposed system across five areas:
- **Technical Feasibility:** Technology availability, required skills, compatibility, and project size.
- **Economic Feasibility:** Development costs, annual costs, expected benefits, ROI, and break-even analysis.
- **Legal and Regulatory Feasibility:** Relevant Jordanian business, electronic transaction, consumer protection, and personal data protection requirements.
- **Desirability:** Business owners' and customers' need for the proposed system.
- **Viability:** The system's suitability for Femmora's business growth and long-term operation.

The report estimates an ROI of 81% and a break-even point of approximately 2.39 years based on its financial assumptions.

## System Analysis

### Development Methodology
Selected **Scrum**, an Agile development methodology, based on the project's need for flexibility, iterative development, and continuous stakeholder feedback.

### Requirement Gathering
Two investigation techniques were used:

**Participant Observation**
- Participated in Femmora's order preparation and delivery operations.
- Observed order management, inventory handling, and customer communication processes.
- Identified operational problems and workflow limitations.

**Questionnaire**
- Collected 31 customer responses.
- Investigated experiences with Instagram shopping.
- Identified customer expectations and desired e-commerce features.

### System Requirements
The report identifies:
- Functional requirements
- Non-functional requirements
- System-level requirements
- User-level requirements
- Organizational and regulatory constraints
